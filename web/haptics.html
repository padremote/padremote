Prefer a click sound?

Sound gives you press and release feedback when vibration is unavailable.

If you hear nothing, check your device’s volume and silent mode. Enable Click sound in the trackpad’s quick settings to use it while dragging.

    Vibration accepted, but nothing happens?

    An accepted result means the browser accepted the request. It does not confirm that the device vibrated.

    1. Check that your device has a vibration motor. Some tablets do not.
    2. In your device’s sound settings, check vibration intensity and touch feedback.
    3. Try an action that normally vibrates elsewhere on your device. If it is also silent, check your device settings.
    4. Use Click sound for press feedback if vibration is unavailable.
